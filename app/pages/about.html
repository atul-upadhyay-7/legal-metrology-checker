About this project

A working prototype built for Kritical Hacks 2026, AI/ML track.

Why it exists

Mandatory declarations are spread across listing descriptions, specification tables and package images. Reviewing them by hand is slow and inconsistent. This tool gives a consistent first pass with the evidence attached.

What it is not

It is not a legal opinion, a certificate of compliance or an enforcement tool. It sends no notices and changes no seller accounts. A person confirms every finding.

Your data

Pasted text and label photos are processed in memory and are not stored by the app. A product URL is sent to TinyFish to fetch the page. Nothing else leaves the server.

Built with

Python and FastAPI, Tesseract OCR, the TinyFish Fetch API for live capture, and anime.js 4.5.0 (MIT licence) for motion, bundled with the app so it works offline.

Where the rules come from

  • Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 as amended: Rule 6(1)(a), (aa), (c), (d), (e), Rule 6(2), Rule 6(10). Checked against the Department of Consumer Affairs consolidated text (December 2024).
  • Department of Consumer Affairs FAQ on the Rules, questions 27 and 50.
  • Amendments after the December 2024 consolidation are not covered and should be checked before relying on a result.
