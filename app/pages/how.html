How it works

How a check works

Plain text matching against six declarations. Every result says what it found and which rule applies.

The pipeline

  1. CaptureOnline listing: the TinyFish Fetch API returns the live page text. Package label: Tesseract OCR reads the photo. Or paste text.
  2. GateA consent page, a bot wall or a page with no price is not a product page. Those become Needs review and no declaration is judged.
  3. CheckSix rules run on the text, in the chosen context (online listing or package label).

The six rules

DeclarationRuleWhat the checker looks for
Net quantity6(1)(c)A value with a standard unit (g, kg, ml, l, pieces).
MRP6(1)(e)An MRP amount with "inclusive of all taxes" next to it.
Manufacture month and year6(1)(d), 6(10)Required on the package. Not required on an online listing.
Manufacturer, packer or importer6(1)(a)A name and address after "Manufactured, Packed, Marketed or Imported by".
Consumer complaint contact6(2)A labelled phone number or email.
Country of origin6(1)(aa)Required for imported goods only, so missing means review.

Online listing vs package label

Online listing

Rule 6(10): the e-commerce display must carry the Rule 6(1) declarations except the month and year of manufacture or packing. So that check is shown as not applicable.

Package label

All six declarations apply. A best-before or use-by date is a separate declaration and never replaces manufacture month and year. Food packages follow the Food Safety and Standards Act for date marking, so a missing date goes to review.

What the statuses mean

Found The declaration is in the text. Potential issue Not found, or incomplete. A person should confirm. Needs review The tool cannot decide. Not applicable The rule does not apply here.

Limits

  • The checks are text patterns. A declaration in a page section the capture missed shows as a potential issue.
  • Net quantity applies to pre-packaged goods. The tool does not decide whether a product is one.
  • Not covered: unit sale price, font size and display panel rules, dimensions, other e-commerce provisions, and amendments after the December 2024 consolidated text.
  • A first-pass aid, not legal advice.
