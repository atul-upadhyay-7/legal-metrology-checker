What it does differently

1

Evidence for every finding

Each result shows the text it matched, the rule reference, and the reason. A finding is a lead for a person to confirm, not a verdict.

2

Online and package are different

Rule 6(10) exempts manufacture month and year from the online display. The checker applies that, and still requires it on the physical package.

3

Live capture with TinyFish

For a listing, the page is fetched live through the TinyFish Fetch API. A page that cannot be read becomes Needs review, never a false violation.

One check, three steps

  1. CaptureURL fetched live, or a label photo read with OCR, or text pasted.
  2. CheckSix declarations tested against Rule 6(1), 6(2) and 6(10).
  3. ReviewSee found text, reasons and rule references. Decide what to fix.
