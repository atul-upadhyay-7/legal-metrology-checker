Legal Metrology Checker

Checks product declarations against the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, and shows the evidence and rule behind every finding.

The page is captured live with TinyFish, then checked under Rule 6(10).

Text is read with OCR, then checked against Rule 6(1) and 6(2).

What gets checked

Six declarations, each tied to its rule.

  • Net quantityRule 6(1)(c)
  • MRP, inclusive of all taxesRule 6(1)(e)
  • Manufacture month and yearRule 6(1)(d), 6(10)
  • Manufacturer, packer or importer addressRule 6(1)(a)
  • Consumer complaint contactRule 6(2)
  • Country of origin (imported goods)Rule 6(1)(aa)
Online vs package. Rule 6(10) exempts manufacture month and year from the online display. A listing is not faulted for it. The physical package still needs it.
First-pass aid, not legal advice. A result of "no issue" covers the checked rules only. A page that could not be read is Needs review, never a violation.
